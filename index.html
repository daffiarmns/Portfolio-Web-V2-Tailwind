How I work

My editing process

A simple workflow that keeps the project organized, communication clear, and revisions easier to manage.

01

Brief & footage

I review the goal, reference, platform, target audience, deadline, and all available footage.

02

Rough cut

I select the strongest moments, build the structure, and establish the main rhythm of the video.

03

Visual & audio polish

Subtitles, B-roll, graphics, music, sound effects, color correction, and final pacing are refined.

04

Review & delivery

Feedback is applied, the final quality is checked, and the video is exported for the required platform.
